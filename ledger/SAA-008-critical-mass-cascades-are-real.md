## Claim
A committed minority sharing the same conviction can, once it reaches a critical
mass, trigger a self-propagating cascade that flips a social system. The grade
splits by layer: **ROBUST for the *mechanism*, QUALIFIED for any *threshold*.**
Two of the four lines are *formal models* showing the mechanism is possible — and
rare: Granovetter threshold models (1978) and Watts network cascades (2002). Two
are *empirical but contested*: Centola's 25% tipping experiment (2018) is lab-only
and not replicated at population scale, and Chenoweth's ~3.5% is a descriptive,
autocracy-scoped regularity with failed counterexamples and a declining nonviolent
success rate. Otto et al. 2020 is a *proposed* intervention framework (expert
elicitation, which drew a PNAS reply) — an agenda, not civilizational-scale
validation. **Read with [`SAA-009`](SAA-009-no-universal-threshold.md): no universal
number, and cascades are not automatic.**

## Evidence
The *mechanism* is well-supported and convergent. The exact threshold and its
guarantees are qualified — see [`SAA-009`](SAA-009-no-universal-threshold.md).
The "energy" is made precise in [`SAA-010`](SAA-010-energy-is-complex-contagion.md).

## Why it matters
This is the world-scale theory of change — and it tells you the lever: build the
*trusted, reinforcing signal* a committed minority can align and coordinate on.
