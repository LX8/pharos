## Claim
Higher-precision studies show *weaker* contact effects; studies with
pre-analysis plans found only 0.016 SD vs 0.451 SD without (meta-regression
intercept ≈ −0.014, i.e. ~zero). The ethnic/racial prejudice most relevant to
political violence is the sparsest and weakest subset (~0.25 SD), and **not one
study** measured interracial contact effects on people older than 25.

## Evidence
Verified 3–0 against the primary source. The authors call their bias tests
"statistically equivocal," so this is a strong directional caution, not proof of
a zero true effect.

## Why it matters
A promising but not-yet-proven lever, least proven exactly for adults and the
prejudice categories that matter most. The REFUTED over-claim — that the
headline effect is *immune* to publication bias — is logged at
[`SAA-011`](SAA-011-refuted-pseudoscience.md)-adjacent reasoning.
