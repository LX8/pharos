## Claim
Whether authoritarian-populist support is driven primarily by *status threat* or
*economic anxiety* is genuinely unresolved. Mutz (2018) attributed rising
support to status threat among traditionally high-status groups; Morgan (2018),
reanalyzing Mutz's own released data, found it "at least as" consistent with
economic interests. A full published exchange leaves it open.

## Evidence
The descriptive panel finding (personal finances had little direct effect) is
uncontested; Mutz's stronger *causal* headline is contested. US-centric (one
2016 case) — do **not** treat status-threat as a global law.

## Why it matters
Interventions premised on a single confident driver are not yet warranted. This
is the political-economy question in miniature, and it is open.
