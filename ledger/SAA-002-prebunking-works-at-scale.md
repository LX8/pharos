## Claim
Short videos/games that pre-expose people to weakened doses of common
manipulation techniques confer "mental antibodies": across seven preregistered
studies (>29,000 participants), including a YouTube field experiment
(n≈22,632), treated users were significantly better at identifying manipulation
techniques (P<0.001).

## Evidence
Verified 3–0 against the primary peer-reviewed source. **Efficacy caveat:** the
field effect was *small* (Cohen's h≈0.09, ~5% absolute gain) and measured
immediate technique-recognition — not durable behavior. Read with
[`SAA-003`](SAA-003-prebunking-transfer-contested.md).

## Why it matters
A real, scalable lever for the *information substrate* — but a lever to **test**,
not a solved cure. Do not overstate it to durable real-world immunity.
