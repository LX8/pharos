## Claim
Prebunking reliably boosts *technique recognition when directly assessed*, but
its transfer to actual true-vs-fake discrimination — and its durability — are
unestablished and sometimes null. A preregistered replication (n=150) found no
significant true/false discrimination gain (dz=0.04; BF₁₀=0.10, favoring the
null); with proper counterbalancing the key prior finding failed to replicate.

## Evidence
Verified 3–0 against a peer-reviewed **null** result (not cherry-picking).

## Why it matters
The honest counterweight to [`SAA-002`](SAA-002-prebunking-works-at-scale.md). A
program shipping prebunking as a deployed "cure" would be ahead of the evidence.
Treat counter-messaging as a hypothesis to A/B test transparently.
