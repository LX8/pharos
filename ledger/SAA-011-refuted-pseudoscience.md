## Claim
The following are **not** supported and would discredit the real critical-mass
science by association:

- **The "100th monkey effect"** — that a behavior spreads instantly/non-locally
  once enough individuals learn it. Fabricated; Lyall Watson admitted he
  "embellished the facts"; the actual macaque behavior spread gradually.
- **Sheldrake's "morphic resonance"** — a non-physical field transmitting ideas.
  Not falsifiable; no empirical support; rejected by mainstream science.
- **"Consciousness energy fields" / the Maharishi effect** — that group
  meditation lowers crime via a field. Crime data contradicts it; cherry-picked;
  no mechanism.

## Evidence
Each refuted in the skeptical and scientific literature.
