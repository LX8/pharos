## Claim
Intergroup contact typically correlates with reduced prejudice: the largest
meta-analysis (Pettigrew & Tropp 2006: 713 samples, 515 studies, 250,089
individuals, 38 nations) found 94% of samples showed an inverse relationship; a
meta-analysis restricted to randomized experiments with delayed outcomes
(Paluck, Green & Green 2019) found an average effect of 0.39 SD (24 of 27
experiments positive).

## Evidence
Verified 3–0 against the two canonical meta-analyses. The descriptive headline
numbers are robust and uncontested — but must be read jointly with
[`SAA-005`](SAA-005-contact-caveats.md).

## Why it matters
A real lever against the intergroup hostility fascism mobilizes — but fragile
exactly where it matters most (see SAA-005).
