# Science Against Authoritarianism — Research Synthesis

**How can science — as a method (falsifiability, reproducibility, open
data) and as an institution — measurably contain political violence and
the growth of fascism / authoritarianism worldwide?**

By The Pharos contributors — a project for the public, for the good of humanity and the environment
· Compiled 2026-06-02 · License CC BY-SA 4.0

> **Provenance.** This report was produced by an adversarial
> deep-research run: 5 search angles → 29 sources fetched → 137 claims
> extracted → 25 claims put to 3-vote verification → **21 confirmed, 4
> refuted** → synthesized to 7 high-confidence findings. It is
> deliberately written to the project's own standard — **cite only what
> is measured** — which means it reports what survived verification and
> is explicit about what did not. Run statistics are in the
> [Method & Provenance](#method--provenance) appendix.

---

## How to read this report

Every substantive claim below carries an evidence grade. This is the
whole point: a defense against a politics that subordinates truth to
power has to hold *itself* to a standard anyone can audit.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **ROBUST** | Verified 3–0 against primary sources; uncontested in the literature |
| ⚠️ **CONTESTED** | Real evidence on both sides; replication failures or unresolved expert disputes |
| ❌ **REFUTED** | Failed adversarial verification in this run — *do not build on it* |
| ❓ **OPEN** | A question the verified evidence does **not** answer |

**A candid scope warning.** Much of the canonical literature this run
went looking for — Chenoweth & Stephan's "3.5%" civil-resistance
finding, Levitsky & Ziblatt, Barbara Walter on anocracy and
factionalism, Stanton's genocide stages, Harff's risk model, the
Cure Violence epidemic model, and the Polanyi/Piketty/Acemoglu
political-economy canon — **did not survive into the verified claim
set** at the confidence this report requires. Their absence here is
*not* evidence against them; it means they were not pinned to a
primary-source, independently-verified quote in this pass. The synthesis
is therefore **partial**, and weighted toward four things that *were*
nailed down: (1) defining fascism, (2) prebunking, (3) contact theory,
and (4) conflict forecasting. Treat the gaps as the backlog for the next
verification round, not as settled silence.

## Executive summary

The evidence supports a defense along **two complementary axes**, and —
importantly — the two are not equally well-supported.

**Axis 1 — Science as method (epistemic defense).** Fascism, defined
rigorously, runs on *mobilizing passions* — manufactured crisis,
victimhood, purity, the cult of the leader — **not** on falsifiable
claims [✅ F1]. That is precisely why open, reproducible, falsifiability-
first knowledge is structurally hostile to it: the antidote to a
movement that treats truth as an instrument of will is knowledge that
anyone can check. Two behavioral tools aim at this affective machinery —
psychological **inoculation / "prebunking"** and **intergroup contact** —
and both show genuine but **contested** effects (below).

**Axis 2 — Science as institution (early warning).** The strongest,
least-contested lever is infrastructural: open, reproducible,
**uncertainty-honest forecasting** of organized violence and democratic
backsliding, built entirely on public datasets. Uppsala's **ViEWS/VIEWS**
is the existence proof [✅ F7]. This axis rests on unanimous,
primary-source evidence — and it happens to be the exact intersection of
what this project can do (reproducible computation, falsifiable modeling,
open data).

**The honest qualifier.** The two most "actionable" behavioral
interventions are the most contested in the literature. Inoculation
boosts *technique recognition* at scale [✅ F2] but **fails to transfer**
reliably to real true-vs-false discrimination and may not be durable
[⚠️ F3]. Contact correlates with lower prejudice [✅ F4] but shows
**publication-bias signatures**, near-null effects in the most rigorous
studies, and **zero** measured effect on interracial prejudice in adults
over 25 [⚠️ F5]. And the headline driver debate — **status threat vs.
economic anxiety** — is genuinely **unresolved** [⚠️ F6].

**Design implication (the spine of Part IV).** Under "cite only what is
measured," the most defensible new initiative leans on the *robust* leg —
open, falsifiable, uncertainty-quantified forecasting and an honest,
living evidence ledger — and treats counter-messaging and contact as
**hypotheses to test transparently**, not as products to ship.

## Part I — Defining the target

You cannot measure what you refuse to define. Loose use of "fascism" as
an epithet is itself a small surrender of the epistemic standard this
project defends.

> **✅ F1 — Fascism is a form of political *behavior*, not a doctrine.**
> Following Robert Paxton's *The Anatomy of Fascism* (Knopf, 2004),
> fascism is marked by "obsessive preoccupation with community decline,
> humiliation, or victimhood" and "compensatory cults of unity, energy,
> and purity," in which a mass-based party of nationalist militants, in
> uneasy but effective collaboration with traditional elites, abandons
> democratic liberties and pursues "redemptive violence … without ethical
> or legal restraints." Paxton identifies a set of **mobilizing
> passions** (overwhelming crisis beyond traditional solutions; the
> primacy of the group; the sense of the group as victim; dread of
> decline; the need for a purer community by exclusionary violence; the
> cult of a natural leader; the beauty of violence and the efficacy of
> will) and models fascism as a **five-stage process** (creation of
> movements → rooting in the political system → seizure of power →
> exercise of power → radicalization or entropy). *Verified 3–0 against
> the primary text* [1].

Two consequences flow from F1 and shape everything downstream:

1. **Fascism is affective, not propositional.** It is, in Paxton's
   phrase, "an affair of the gut more than of the brain." This is the
   load-bearing reason the *epistemic* defense matters: open science
   does not refute fascism by winning an argument (it makes few
   falsifiable arguments); it **denies fascism the cover of manufactured
   ignorance** and gives institutions and publics auditable ground to
   stand on.
2. **Definitions are contested — so anchor, don't checklist.** Paxton's
   is the rigorous anchor, but Griffin (palingenetic ultranationalism),
   Eco (*Ur-Fascism*'s 14 features [2]), Mann, and Stanley [3] differ.
   Paxton *himself* warns against naïve checklist scoring; most movements
   never complete all five stages. Any early-warning tool must treat the
   stages as **probabilistic states**, not a scorecard.

## Part II — The four lenses, graded by evidence

### Lens 1 — Epistemic defense / open science

> **✅ F2 — Psychological inoculation ("prebunking") can improve
> resistance to manipulation at scale.** Short videos or games that
> pre-expose people to weakened doses of common manipulation techniques
> (impersonation, emotional language, false dichotomy, scapegoating,
> conspiratorial reasoning) confer "mental antibodies." Across **seven
> preregistered studies** (six RCTs, n = 6,464; plus a YouTube field
> experiment, n = 22,632; >29,000 total), treated users were
> significantly better at identifying manipulation techniques —
> including a real-world platform field experiment (n ≈ 11,432 vs.
> 11,200) at P < 0.001. *Verified 3–0* [4].

> **⚠️ F3 — …but it does NOT reliably transfer to real true-vs-fake
> discrimination, and may not be durable.** A preregistered replication
> (n = 150) of the *Bad News* game found **no** significant improvement
> in discriminating true from fake headlines (dz = 0.04; Bayesian
> evidence favoring the null, BF₁₀ = 0.10); with proper counterbalancing,
> the key prior finding failed to replicate. The field effect in F2 was
> **small** (Cohen's h ≈ 0.09; ~5% absolute gain) and measured immediate,
> within-24h *technique recognition* — not durable behavior, not voting,
> not sharing. *Verified 3–0 against a peer-reviewed null result* [6].

**Read F2 and F3 together:** inoculation reliably boosts *technique
recognition when you directly test for it*; its transfer to real-world
discernment and its persistence over months are **unestablished and
sometimes null**. A program that ships prebunking as a deployed "cure"
would be **ahead of the evidence**.

> **❌ REFUTED — "Prebunking generalizes across cultures with minimal
> variance."** The claim that the *Bad News* effect is essentially
> culture-invariant (intraclass correlation ≈ 0.001) **failed
> verification 0–3** [5]. Do not assume an intervention validated in one
> context transfers to another; that assumption is itself a measurement
> error.

The deeper Lens-1 point is institutional, not gadget-level: the
**reproducibility movement** (open data, preregistration, independent
replication — e.g. *The Turing Way* [17]) is a *civic* template. The same
machinery that catches a p-hacked psychology result is what catches a
manufactured political "fact." **Epistemic security** — protecting a
society's information supply chain — is now an explicit research program
[18], and the 2024–2026 election cycle made the threat concrete: AI-
enabled influence operations are documented and growing [24][25][26],
even as V-Dem and Freedom House record a record run of democratic
backsliding [21][22][23].

### Lens 2 — Social science of violence & democratic backsliding

> **✅ F4 — Intergroup contact typically correlates with reduced
> prejudice.** The largest meta-analysis (Pettigrew & Tropp 2006: 713
> samples, 515 studies, 250,089 individuals, 38 nations) found **94%** of
> samples showed an inverse contact–prejudice relationship; a meta-
> analysis restricted to randomized experiments with delayed outcomes
> (Paluck, Green & Green 2019) found an average effect of **0.39 SD** (24
> of 27 experiments positive). *Verified 3–0* [7][8].

> **⚠️ F5 — …but the contact evidence carries serious publication-bias
> and external-validity caveats.** In Paluck et al. 2019, higher-
> precision studies show *weaker* effects; studies with **pre-analysis
> plans** found only **0.016 SD** vs. 0.451 SD without (the meta-
> regression intercept for an infinitely precise study is ≈ −0.014, i.e.
> ~zero). The ethnic/racial prejudice most relevant to political violence
> is the *sparsest and weakest* subset (~0.25 SD), and **not one study
> measured interracial contact effects on people older than 25**.
> *Verified 3–0* [8]. (The authors call their bias tests "statistically
> equivocal," so this is a strong directional caution, not proof of a
> zero true effect.)

> **❌ REFUTED — "Contact's r ≈ −.21 is not attributable to publication
> bias."** The specific claim that the headline contact effect is
> *immune* to selection/publication bias **failed 0–3** [7]. The honest
> reading is F4 + F5: a real but fragile, possibly over-estimated lever,
> least proven exactly where it would matter most.

> **⚠️ F6 — The driver of authoritarian-populist support (status threat
> vs. economic anxiety) is genuinely UNRESOLVED.** Mutz (PNAS 2018),
> using a nationally representative panel tracking the *same* individuals
> 2012→2016, found change in personal finances had little effect on
> candidate preference and attributed rising support to **status
> threat** among traditionally high-status groups. Morgan (Socius 2018),
> reanalyzing **Mutz's own released data**, found the data "at least as"
> consistent with economic interests mattering, and that relative
> importance cannot be cleanly estimated here. A full published exchange
> (Mutz reply → Morgan rejoinder) leaves it **open**. *Verified;
> descriptive panel finding uncontested, Mutz's causal headline
> contested.* This is **US-centric** (a single 2016 case) — **do not
> treat status-threat as a global law** [9][10][11].

**The honest state of Lens 2:** the *descriptive* facts are solid; the
*causal and policy* claims are where the field actually argues. And much
of the canon the brief asked for (Chenoweth, Walter, Stanton, Harff)
is **❓ OPEN** here — not verified in this pass.

### Lens 3 — Systems & technology

> **✅ F7 — Operational, openly-reproducible conflict early-warning
> systems already exist — and are the strongest institutional model.**
> Uppsala's **ViEWS** (operational since June 2018) publishes
> monthly-updated forecasts of organized violence up to **36 months**
> ahead, at country and subnational (PRIO-GRID cell) resolution, across
> all three UCDP violence types. It integrates **open** datasets
> (UCDP-GED, monthly UCDP-Candidate, PRIO-GRID, World Development
> Indicators, ACLED protests, V-Dem institutions), with publicly
> published forecasts and open replication code (OpenViEWS). Its
> successor **VIEWS** forecasts the *number* of conflict fatalities and —
> crucially — treats **uncertainty quantification as a first-class
> requirement** (probabilistic submissions scored by CRPS), explicitly
> because point predictions are uninformative precisely when early
> warning matters most. *Verified 3–0 against primary sources and live
> infrastructure* [12][13][14].

> **❌ REFUTED — "Gunshot violence spreads as a measurable epidemic
> contagion."** The claim that most Chicago shootings are attributable to
> social-network transmission rather than demographic risk **failed
> verification 0–3** [16]. **Do not build on an "epidemic model of
> violence" as if it were established** — the metaphor is popular and
> unproven at the strength claimed.

The actionable Lens-3 lesson: **interoperate, don't reinvent.** ViEWS/
VIEWS, UCDP, ACLED [15], and V-Dem already define the open standards. The
additive move is a *new layer* on top of them, not a competitor — and the
discipline to copy is VIEWS's **uncertainty-first, CRPS-scored,
fully-replicable** design. AI here is genuinely dual-use: the same
modeling that powers early warning powers influence operations [24][25].

### Lens 4 — Political economy & institutions

This is the thinnest verified lens. The political-economy canon
(Polanyi's *Great Transformation*, Piketty on inequality, Acemoglu &
Robinson) did **not** survive into the verified claim set in this pass —
**❓ OPEN**, flagged for the next round. The one verified touchpoint is
**F6**'s status-threat-vs-economic-anxiety dispute, which is itself the
political-economy question in miniature and is **unresolved**. The
honest position: the report cannot, from *this* evidence, adjudicate
which structural conditions starve or feed authoritarian movements. It
can only mark the question as live and important.

## Part III — What the evidence implies

Stack the grades and a clear instruction emerges:

- **Build on the robust leg.** Rigorous definition (F1) + open,
  uncertainty-honest forecasting (F7) rest on unanimous, primary-source
  evidence. This is where to put weight.
- **Test, don't ship, the contested leg.** Inoculation (F2/F3) and
  contact (F4/F5) are real but fragile. The right posture is a
  **transparent A/B testing harness**, with preregistration and honest
  reporting of nulls — *not* a deployed "solution."
- **Refuse the refuted leg.** No epidemic-contagion model of violence;
  no assumption that prebunking or any intervention generalizes across
  contexts; no treating contact's headline effect as bias-free.
- **Hold the open questions open.** Status-threat vs. economics; durable
  prebunking transfer; adult interracial contact; and — the design crux —
  how to make an open early-warning tool **net-protective rather than
  net-enabling** for would-be repressors.

This is itself the thesis in miniature: the discipline that grades its
own claims honestly is the discipline fascism cannot practice.

## Part IV — Design of a new initiative

> **PHAROS** — *an open, falsifiable early-warning observatory for
> democratic resilience.* The initiative's name; the umbrella project is
> *Science Against Authoritarianism* (repository: `LX8/pharos`). "Pharos" = the lighthouse of
> Alexandria: a beacon that **warns without targeting** and is **visible
> to everyone** — the opposite of a surveillance tower.

### Mission

Convert the social science of democratic backsliding into **open,
reproducible, uncertainty-quantified, falsifiable forecasts and an
honest living record of what is actually known** — pitched at the level
of **institutions and the conduct of power, never individuals** — so
that civil society, journalists, and accountability actors get earlier,
checkable warning, and so that the evidence base itself stays honest.

### The gap it fills (additive, not duplicative)

ViEWS/VIEWS forecasts *organized violence* (fatalities) brilliantly
[F7]. Pharos deliberately does **not** rebuild that. It targets two gaps
ViEWS leaves open:

1. **The backsliding layer *upstream* of violence.** Most democratic
   erosion in 2024–2026 is not mass killing; it is the quiet dismantling
   of checks, courts, press, and electoral integrity [21][22][23].
   Pharos forecasts **institutional-backsliding events and Paxton-stage
   transitions** (F1, as probabilistic states), using V-Dem / Freedom
   House / ACLED-protest signals — a complementary target to ViEWS's
   fatality forecasts, sharing its data standards.
2. **The epistemic layer.** No early-warning system tracks *the state of
   its own evidence base*. Pharos adds an **open evidence ledger** that
   grades interventions ROBUST / CONTESTED / REFUTED / OPEN (exactly as
   this report does) and **updates as replications land** — the
   institutional answer to Lens 1.

### Architecture — two interlocking pillars

**Pillar A — `pharos-forecast` (the robust leg).**
- **Inputs:** public datasets only — V-Dem, Freedom House, ACLED, UCDP,
  PRIO-GRID, World Bank WDI. No proprietary, no person-level data
  (a hard architectural floor — see Risks).
- **Targets:** probabilistic forecasts of backsliding indicators
  (e.g. V-Dem component declines crossing pre-registered thresholds) and
  movement→regime stage transitions, at **country / sub-national** but
  **never sub-group or individual** resolution.
- **Method:** copy VIEWS's discipline — **uncertainty as a first-class
  output**, scored by **CRPS** against realized outcomes; every forecast
  **pre-registered and timestamped**; full open replication
  (GPL-3.0 code, CC0 derived data).
- **Falsifiability:** each forecast carries an explicit **scoring rule
  and a "this model is wrong if…" condition**. Models that miss are
  downgraded in public, on the record.

**Pillar B — `pharos-ledger` (the epistemic leg).**
- A versioned, openly-licensed registry of **what is known about
  containing authoritarianism**, each entry graded and **each carrying a
  pre-registered falsification trigger** (the condition under which it
  would be downgraded). Seeded by *this report*: 7 findings, 4 refuted
  claims, 4 open questions.
- Maintained by the **same adversarial-verification method** that
  produced this report (multi-vote, refute-by-default), run as recurring
  infrastructure.
- This is the antidote to *both* failure modes: disinformation **and**
  well-meaning but unreplicated "solutions" (it would have caught the
  prebunking replication failure and the refuted epidemic claim *before*
  anyone built on them).

### Minimal viable first version (shippable, small, honest)

1. **`pharos-ledger` v0** — this report rendered as a structured,
   versioned evidence base (one file per finding, SPDX-licensed,
   REUSE-compliant), each with its grade, sources, and falsification
   trigger. *No modeling required; pure open-science infrastructure;
   immediately useful.*
2. **`pharos-forecast` v0** — **one** well-scoped, pre-registered
   forecast: pick a single V-Dem liberal-democracy sub-indicator,
   forecast its 12-month change for a small set of countries with an
   honest predictive interval, register it publicly, and **score it
   against the next V-Dem release by CRPS**. One falsifiable prediction,
   fully reproduced end-to-end.

### How it plugs in

Pharos consumes and cites ViEWS/UCDP/ACLED/V-Dem under their own
licenses (it relicenses nothing — see `LICENSE`), interoperates with
their formats, and contributes *back* a backsliding-forecast layer and a
public evidence ledger. Additive by construction.

## Part V — Risks & limits

The brief demanded a clear-eyed account. Here it is.

- **Dual-use is intrinsic, not incidental.** *The same risk map that
  warns an NGO can help a regime target dissidents.* This is the central
  hazard, and it is why Pharos forecasts **power and institutions, not
  people**: a hard architectural floor against person- or sub-group-level
  resolution, no individual features, public-interest aggregation only.
  Open question #4 — *how to make openness net-protective, not
  net-enabling* — is not solved; it is a **standing research and
  governance obligation**, encoded in the `NOTICE` ethical-use covenant.
- **Predictive tooling can entrench the harm it claims to fight.**
  Predictive policing is the cautionary tale: "good guys" using the bad
  guys' tools produced biased, rights-eroding systems [19]. Forecasting
  the *conduct of power-holders* is a different target than forecasting
  *populations* — but the line must be defended in code and governance,
  not just in intent.
- **Techno-solutionism is a real failure mode.** Morozov's critique
  applies directly [20]: a dashboard does not defeat fascism. F1 is
  explicit that fascism is driven by passions and political economy that
  **no model fixes**. Pharos is an *instrument for humans doing political
  and civic work* — early warning, accountability, honest evidence — not
  a substitute for it.
- **The evidence is thin and contested exactly where action is most
  tempting.** Inoculation and contact — the two "do something now"
  levers — are the **least** robust (F3, F5). Building policy on them as
  if settled would repeat the error this whole method exists to prevent.
- **Open science lowers the barrier for abuse as well as for research.**
  Publishing models and data is a value *and* a risk. The mitigation is
  the deliberate choice of *target* (institutions/power, aggregate) over
  *capability* (population, granular), plus the covenant in `NOTICE`.
- **What science cannot do.** It cannot supply the courage of the 3.5%,
  the judgment of a free press, the solidarity that protects a targeted
  neighbor, or the political choices a society must make. Science can
  **define the target honestly, warn earlier, keep the evidence base
  clean, and refuse to lie.** That is a real contribution. It is not the
  whole fight, and pretending otherwise would be its own small fascism of
  the spreadsheet.

## Open questions (the verified frontier)

1. **Durability & transfer of prebunking** — does inoculation produce
   months-long protection and transfer to real sharing/voting, or only
   immediate technique-recognition? (The 2022 scale-efficacy vs. 2025
   replication-failure tension [4][6].)
2. **Status threat vs. economic anxiety** — unresolved on US-2016 data
   [9][10][11]; no global/comparative resolution in the verified set.
3. **Adult, interracial, at-scale contact** — does contact causally
   reduce the prejudice most relevant to violence among people >25? The
   2019 meta-analysis found *zero* such studies [8].
4. **Net-protective openness (the design crux)** — what governance,
   access-control, and licensing model makes an open early-warning tool
   demonstrably more useful to defenders than to repressors? Pharos is an
   attempt to *operationalize* this question, not a claim to have
   answered it.
